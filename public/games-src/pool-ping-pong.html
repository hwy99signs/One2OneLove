Before you start: lay the phone flat where you'll play and tap 🎯 Set flat (in Settings). That sets YOUR flat as level, so the ball stays still when the phone is flat. Skip it and the game uses the phone's true flat — on a tilted surface the ball will drift.
Put the phone on a flat surface, screen up, then angle the phone left, right, back or forth or swipe to roll the 8-ball. Any slight angle moves it; a bigger angle rolls it faster.
Middle hole wins — the two middle pockets have permanent gold rims: those are the good pots and the only ones that count. The 6 outer red pockets are fouls.
Only one hole is open at a time — it closes and another opens at the Hole Speed slider in the Settings grid. Covered holes are safe to roll over.
Your ball is the 8-ball. The angry red ball, bigger than your ball, roams the table and will knock your ball — it runs at twice your sensitivity speed, and it can never fall in a hole. Every 3 balls you pot, another angry red ball joins the table — up to 3 of them. Only your 8-ball can fall in a hole.
Scoring: Each pot scores 100 × your current streak. A golden hole pays double.
Modes: Classic: one foul ends your run. 60-Second Challenge: a foul costs 5 seconds and your streak — play continues until the clock runs out.
Golden holes vs. gold rims: The GOLDEN HOLES feature makes a random OPEN hole golden for double points (25% chance per open when enabled). This is different from the permanent gold RIMS on the two middle pockets.
The feature settings above set your game: 🏃 Speed ramp makes the holes close faster after each win · ✨ Golden holes can double points · 🎲 Moving start puts the ball in a new spot each round · 😡 Red balls can be switched off for a clear table.
