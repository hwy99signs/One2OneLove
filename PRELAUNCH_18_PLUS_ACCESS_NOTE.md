# PRELAUNCH NOTE — 18+ ACCESS & MATURE-CONTENT SAFEGUARDS

Status: **Required before launch review**

This note is intentionally stored on the O2OL prelaunch branch so the requirement is not forgotten. It is a planning reminder only and does **not** by itself implement an age gate.

## Required platform rule

One2OneLove should be treated as an **18+ platform** because O2OL may include adult relationship discussions, including intimacy, sexuality, infidelity, sexual health, and other mature relationship topics.

Before production promotion, confirm and implement:

1. **18+ entry gate before guest preview or signup**
   - Users must confirm they are at least 18 before entering the site, including the 24-hour guest preview.
   - Preferred implementation: age/date-of-birth screen plus acceptance of Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
   - Do not rely on a video disclaimer alone.

2. **Account eligibility**
   - Terms of Service should state that accounts are for users age 18+ only.
   - Signup flow should consistently enforce the same rule.

3. **O2OL Studio / video content notices**
   - General relationship episodes can use a discreet “18+” indicator where appropriate.
   - Episodes with explicit sexual/intimacy subject matter should include a stronger mature-content notice before playback.
   - Mature-content notices should be based on the actual subject matter. Discussion of transgender people or gender identity is not inherently sexual or adult content.

4. **Suggested entry copy**
   - “One2OneLove is intended for adults age 18 and older and may include discussions about relationships, intimacy, sexuality, and other mature topics. By continuing, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old and agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.”

5. **Launch-readiness check**
   - Verify the age gate appears before the 24-hour guest preview.
   - Verify the age rule is reflected consistently in Terms, Privacy, signup, and O2OL Studio playback.
   - Legal review should be completed before final production launch.

## Important

Do **not** merge or deploy this requirement to Production without explicit approval. Implement and test in Prelaunch first.
